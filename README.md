# SEP11 Freedom Project
by **X**

## Context
I am a student at [HSTAT](https://www.hstat.org/) in the [Software Engineering Program](https://hstatsep.github.io/). The "Freedom Project" for SEP11 is a year-long project all about making something using JavaScript along with a third-party JS tool.

### I decided to research Kaboom on my own for my project so that I could create Midnight Skater. I used colorful backgrounds and vintage music with the goal of evoking nostalgia so that the player would want to play repeatedly. Over the course of several months, we studied Javascript and a variety of commands that could be useful for our projects. Using blogs, I was able to keep a kind of journal in which I could record when I learnt anything and whether it would be useful for my final project. With learning Kaboom, it allowed me to portray my vision via code. Allowing me to add audio which boosted the engagement to my project.

## Implications
##### PROS
* By skipping lengthy tutorials, the game allows players to immediately submerge themselves in the action. This deep focus helps real world stressors and worries fade away temporarily.
* The tight feedback loop of dodging obstacles and climbing a high scoreboard provides a healthy release of dopamine, rewarding players for their mastery and persistence.
##### CONS
* Because the mechanics rely on high concentration reflex testing, the rapid scaling of difficulty can lead to short-term frustration when a player fails to beat a high score.
* Due to the minimalist nature of classic arcade loops, the gameplay can feel repetitive over extended periods if modern progression systems (like unlockable characters or level varieties) are missing.
